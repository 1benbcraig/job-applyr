How to run this

  1. Click Pick folder and choose ~/Desktop/job-applyr-profile.
  2. In Chrome's prompt, tick “Allow on every visit”, then Allow.
  3. Quit Chrome completely — ⌘Q, not just closing the window.
  4. Reopen Chrome, come back to this page, and read the big box above.
